Password Safety Guidelines

Follow these simple practices to protect your accounts and personal information.

  1. 1. Use long passwords

    Choose a password that is at least 15 characters long when the service allows it. Longer passwords are generally harder to guess.

  2. 2. Use unique passwords

    Never reuse the same password for multiple accounts. If one account is compromised, other accounts remain better protected.

  3. 3. Prefer passphrases

    Consider a long, memorable combination of unrelated words, or use a password manager to generate and store unique passwords.

  4. 4. Enable multi-factor authentication

    Turn on MFA or two-step verification whenever it is available.

  5. 5. Avoid predictable information

    Do not use easily guessed details such as your name, birthday, common words, or predictable sequences.

  6. 6. Stay alert for phishing

    Never enter your password through suspicious links. Check the website address before signing in.
